# Private Reporting and Appeals

Use the private ticket route in this channel to report misconduct, appeal a material moderation action, or raise a governance concern. Do not post reports or public accusations in general channels.

Include what happened, when it happened, who was involved, and any relevant links, screenshots, or other context you are comfortable sharing. Mark raids, compromised accounts, credible threats, doxxing, or active harassment as urgent.

Reports are handled confidentially as far as practical. A report involving a Moderator, Councilor, or the Archivist must be routed so that person does not participate in its review. Retaliation for a good-faith report is prohibited.

The server is not an emergency service. For immediate danger, contact local emergency services or the relevant platform reporting channel.
