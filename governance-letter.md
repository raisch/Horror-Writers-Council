# Founding Governance Review

Hello prospective Councilors and members,

Attached is the proposed founding Constitution for the Horror Writers Council. It establishes how this server will make decisions, choose and hold leaders accountable, protect member safety and creative work, manage conflicts of interest, and revise its rules as the community grows.

This is a request for review, not a request to endorse every future policy in advance. The Constitution sets the durable framework: member rights, Council authority and limits, elections, moderation and appeals, commercial disclosure, financial stewardship, amendment, and dissolution. It is intended to keep the server from depending on any one person's preferences or continued availability.

Some practical details should remain ordinary rules rather than constitutional text. After ratification, the first Council should publish these for member comment:

- Channel structure, access controls, and content-warning conventions.
- Workshop submission, critique-consent, and feedback-boundary procedures.
- Moderator selection, training, documentation, and review procedures.
- The ranked-choice ballot tool and published single transferable vote counting method.
- Commercial-post channels, disclosure format, and posting-frequency limits.
- Financial approval thresholds and the records-retention schedule.

## Review Questions

Please review the Constitution with the following questions in mind:

1. Does it clearly protect members while allowing serious horror craft discussion and critique?
2. Are the Council's powers balanced by sufficient transparency, recusal, removal, and election safeguards?
3. Are the moderation and appeal procedures fair, workable, and safe for people reporting harm?
4. Are any provisions too rigid for a new, small server, or too vague to apply consistently?
5. Is there a material issue the founding document fails to address?

## Proposed Process

1. Keep the draft available for at least 14 days of public comment.
2. Collect proposed amendments in a dedicated governance channel or private Council contact route.
3. Publish a revision log and a final ratification draft that explains which substantive suggestions were adopted or declined.
4. Hold the founding ratification ballot according to Article IX of the Constitution.
5. If ratified, convene the first Council and begin the ordinary-rule process above.

Please focus feedback on concrete text, consequences, and workable alternatives. Good-faith disagreement is part of building a durable community; personal attacks, campaign pressure, and off-server conflict are not.

Thank you for helping establish a horror-writing community worthy of its members' work and trust.

*The Founding Team*
