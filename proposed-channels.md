# Proposed Channel List

This structure keeps essential rules, reporting, governance, workshops, and commercial activity distinct. Channel descriptions, permissions, posting limits, content-warning conventions, and AI-use rules should be adopted as ordinary rules after member comment.

## The Threshold

Read-only or tightly moderated onboarding channels.

- `#welcome-to-the-house` - Server introduction and orientation.
- `#start-here` - Joining steps, role selection, and where to find things.
- `#community-standards` - Community Standards, member rights, and conduct expectations.
- `#announcements` - Official Council, moderation, and server-wide announcements.
- `#questions-for-the-keepers` - Public questions about rules, access, or server operations.
- `#contact-the-council` - Private ticket entry point for reports, appeals, governance concerns, and urgent safety issues.

## The Commons

Everyday conversation for members in good standing.

- `#the-flickering-lamp` - General conversation and introductions.
- `#what-are-you-writing` - Check-ins, works in progress, goals, and milestones.
- `#horror-reading-room` - Books, stories, criticism, and spoiler-marked discussion.
- `#screen-and-scream` - Horror film, television, audio drama, and games discussion.
- `#the-research-cabinet` - Historical, cultural, scientific, and folkloric research; warnings required for disturbing real-world material.
- `#quiet-company` - Low-pressure writing sprints, accountability, and co-working.

## The Workroom

Craft conversation and clearly scoped, opt-in creative exchange.

- `#craft-after-dark` - Technique, structure, voice, pacing, and genre craft discussion.
- `#the-prompt-crypt` - Writing prompts and prompt responses.
- `#brainstorming-seance` - Premise, plot, character, and worldbuilding help.
- `#first-sightings` - Voluntary excerpts and early-draft sharing; no critique unless the author asks for it.
- `#critique-requests` - Requests for feedback with required scope, content warnings, and feedback boundaries.
- `#critique-exchange` - Workshop discussion for accepted submissions; access and posting rules set per workshop cycle.
- `#revision-table` - Revision questions, progress, and post-critique support.
- `#the-unquiet-shelf` - Completed or published member work shared with the author's stated permissions.

## The Red Room

Age-gated, opt-in channels with clear warning and access rules. No channel permits content prohibited by Discord policy or the Community Standards.

- `#mature-horror-craft` - Discussion of adult themes, sexuality, extreme violence, and taboo in horror craft.
- `#restricted-workshop` - Mature workshop submissions and critique under explicit content-warning and consent requirements.
- `#content-warning-desk` - Guidance on warnings, boundaries, and suitable places for difficult material; can remain public and moderator-led.

## The Notice Board

Professional and community opportunities, kept separate from creative conversation.

- `#calls-from-the-dark` - Calls for submissions, anthologies, contests, residencies, and fellowships.
- `#market-watch` - Publishing news, market information, and submission resources.
- `#member-publications` - Clearly labeled releases, readings, crowdfunding, and other member promotion, subject to frequency limits.
- `#services-and-collaborators` - Clearly labeled paid services and collaboration offers, subject to disclosure and posting rules.
- `#events-in-the-lantern-room` - Workshops, readings, book clubs, writing sessions, and event details.

## The Council Chamber

Governance spaces. Public channels should be readable by members; Council and moderation work needs appropriate private access.

- `#governance-hall` - Proposed ordinary rules, constitutional amendments, and member comment.
- `#council-record` - Public agendas, decisions, monthly summaries, election results, financial summaries, and revision logs.
- `#election-booth` - Temporary, read-only channel for candidate statements and questions during election periods.
- `#council-chamber` - Private Council deliberation and decision records.
- `#moderator-watch` - Private moderation coordination, material-action documentation, and training resources.
- `#archivists-workbench` - Private technical administration, security, bot, backup, and recovery planning.

## Optional Voice Channels

- `The Lantern Room` - Casual conversation.
- `The Night Workshop` - Writing sprints and co-working.
- `The Reading` - Readings, book clubs, and scheduled events.
- `Council Meeting Room` - Private Council meetings.

## Recommended Access Model

- Make `#welcome-to-the-house`, `#start-here`, `#community-standards`, and `#announcements` read-only for ordinary members.
- Require an 18+ acknowledgment and rules acceptance before access to community and workroom channels.
- Make `#mature-horror-craft` and `#restricted-workshop` opt-in, age-gated roles with channel-specific content-warning rules.
- Keep `#contact-the-council` as a private ticket system rather than a public discussion channel; ensure reports about a Councilor, Moderator, or Archivist bypass that person.
- Limit `#critique-exchange` to the relevant workshop cohort when confidentiality or submission volume requires it.
- Restrict the Council, moderation, and technical channels to their respective roles, following least-privilege access.
- Open `#election-booth` only during elections and archive or lock it after results are published.
