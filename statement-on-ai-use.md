All use of generative AI on stories is prohibited. This includes using LLMs to generate any part of your story, revise any part of your story, or provide a critique of your story. Simple spell check programs such as those found in MS Word or Google docs are allowed. Additionally, feeding another writer’s story into an LLM to generate a critique or feedback is prohibited. Any use of generative AI as described above will result in removal from the Discord server. If you suspect AI use, inform a moderator via private DM. Public accusations are not allowed in the server.
