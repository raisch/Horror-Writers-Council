# Community Standards

This server is an 18+ community for people who write, read, discuss, draft, revise, and publish horror. We value rigorous creative work, candid but consensual critique, and a space where members can take artistic risks without giving up safety or dignity.

These standards apply in the server, in server-organized activities, and in direct messages or off-server conduct when it uses server membership to target, pressure, exploit, or endanger another member. The [Server Governance & Constitution](./SERVER%20GOVERNANCE%20%26%20CONSTITUTION.md) governs formal process, member rights, and appeals.

## Core Expectations

- Treat other members as people, not as an audience, a source of free labor, or a target.
- Discuss work seriously. Critique ideas, choices, and pages, not a writer's worth or identity.
- Ask for and respect consent. Do not give critique unless it is invited, and honor the writer's stated feedback boundaries.
- Use the appropriate channel and relevant content warnings for disturbing material.
- Respect privacy, creative ownership, and the limits other members set around their time, work, and personal information.
- Disagree in good faith. Do not turn a craft disagreement into a campaign, pile-on, or off-server feud.

<!-- discord-message-break -->

## Conduct We Do Not Permit

Do not:

- Harass, threaten, stalk, discriminate against, or retaliate against anyone.
- Deliberately or repeatedly misgender, deadname, or deny a member's stated identity after correction.
- Dox someone, share private messages or personal information without permission, or seek personal information for coercive purposes.
- Post illegal content, credible threats, sexual content involving minors, or material prohibited by Discord's Terms of Service.
- Plagiarize, impersonate another person, misrepresent authorship, or run scams.
- Flood channels, disrupt workshops or events, evade moderation, or recruit members into unrelated off-server disputes.
- Use the server to promote real-world cruelty, targeted bigotry, or shock for its own sake.
- Send uninvited commercial direct messages based on server membership.

## Horror Content and Content Warnings

Horror is broad. Supernatural, realistic, quiet, grotesque, intimate, and cosmic work all belong here. Horror may engage violence, grief, sex, death, trauma, taboo, and the unknown. Depiction is not endorsement, but intent does not erase impact or remove the need for context and consent.

When sharing material that may affect readers, use the content-warning format required by the channel. Place especially graphic, sexual, or otherwise restricted material only in the channels designated for it. Follow channel-specific limits for violence, sexuality, spoilers, research, and workshop submissions.

Real-world graphic imagery, targeted bigotry, sexual violence presented for shock, and content that creates a credible risk to members may be restricted or removed. No member is entitled to post any material in every space, or to demand that another member engage with it.

## Use of Generative AI

Generative AI may not be used to create, develop, revise, transform, or provide critique of any creative work shared, submitted, critiqued, performed, promoted, or otherwise represented through the server. This includes generated prose, imagery, audio, plot or character development, substantive revision, and AI-generated feedback. Non-generative spelling, grammar, formatting, speech-to-text, and text-to-speech tools are permitted only when they do not generate or substantially rewrite expressive content.

Do not feed another writer's work, critique, workshop discussion, private message, or other server material into an AI system. This is prohibited even when the stated purpose is feedback, summarization, research, or evaluation.

Violations may result in proportionate moderation action, including a permanent ban in serious or repeated cases. If you suspect a violation, inform a Moderator through private direct message or submit a ticket to the Council of Elders.

Public accusations are not allowed. Reports must be handled through the private process in accordance with the Constitution's investigation, confidentiality, and appeal rules.

## Workshops, Critique, and Creative Rights

Share only work you have the right to share. A writer keeps ownership of their draft, research, and critique exchange. Material shared for a workshop may be read and discussed for that workshop; it may not be copied, reposted, distributed, published, adapted, submitted elsewhere, or used outside the server without express permission from the rights holder.

Do not use another member's draft, critique, or workshop discussion to train, evaluate, or improve an AI system without express permission from every affected rights holder. Such a violation may directly affect a writer's ability to make a living and can result in a permanent ban.

Critique should be specific, useful, and directed toward the work. The writer may set the scope of feedback, decline critique, or withdraw their work. Disagreement about a critique does not authorize personal attacks.

## Promotion, Opportunities, and Commercial Interests

You may share your own publications, paid services, crowdfunding campaigns, calls for submissions, and relevant professional opportunities only in designated channels and under their posting-frequency and disclosure rules. Label commercial posts clearly.

Do not use member lists, workshop participation, or private messages to solicit clients, customers, backers, recruits, or followers without an explicit invitation from the recipient. A disclosure does not make an otherwise prohibited promotion acceptable.

Councilors and Moderators must disclose relevant financial interests and step away from related decisions. Members should report undisclosed conflicts or misleading promotions through the private reporting route.

## Privacy and Safety

Do not share a member's private communication, personal information, identity details, location, workplace, or other sensitive information without permission. Do not pressure someone to disclose personal history, trauma, identity, contact information, or unpublished work.

If you believe someone faces an immediate threat, doxxing, account compromise, or active harassment, report it as urgent through `#contact-the-council`. The server is not an emergency service. For immediate danger, contact local emergency services or the relevant platform reporting channel.

<!-- discord-message-break -->

## Reporting and Enforcement

Use `#contact-the-council` and its private ticket system to report misconduct, appeal a material moderation action, or raise a governance concern. A report involving a Moderator, Councilor, or the Archivist must be handled without that person participating in the review.

Moderation responses are proportionate to the conduct, context, prior history, risk, and impact on the community. They may include guidance, a warning, channel restriction, timeout, suspension, role removal, or a ban. Immediate temporary action may be taken to stop active harm.

Reports are treated as confidential as far as practical. Information may be shared only when needed to investigate, protect members, meet platform or legal obligations, or provide a fair appeal. Retaliation for a good-faith report is prohibited.

Members may appeal suspensions of seven days or longer, permanent role removals, and bans within 14 days. See the Constitution for the full appeal procedure.

## A Final Standard

The question is not whether a member can find the narrowest reading of a rule that permits conduct. The question is whether their conduct helps create and foster a serious, safe, and sustainable horror-writing community. When in doubt, choose clarity, consent, privacy, and care for the work.
