# Change Log

This log records substantive changes to the server's governance, standards, member guidance, and planning artifacts. Routine formatting corrections and non-substantive edits are not recorded.

## 2026-09-03 - Seed-message boundaries added

Added explicit author-controlled Discord post boundaries for managed seed content.

- Seed documents can now use `<!-- discord-message-break -->` to start a new Discord post.
- Each explicit section still observes Discord's 2,000-character message limit.

## 2026-08-31 - Initial artifacts created

Created the initial planning and governance artifacts for the Horror Writers Guild:

- `SERVER GOVERNANCE & CONSTITUTION.md`
- `community-standards.md`
- `governance-letter.md`
- `member-guide.md`
- `proposed-names.md`
- `proposed-channels.md`

## 2026-08-31 - Generative-AI authorship prohibition adopted

Adopted a human-only policy for creative work shared, submitted, critiqued, performed, promoted, or otherwise represented through the server.

- Defined `Creative work` and `Generative AI` in the Constitution.
- Prohibited generative AI for creating, developing, revising, transforming, or critiquing covered creative work, including generated prose, imagery, audio, plot and character development, substantive revision, and AI-generated feedback.
- Preserved defined non-generative spelling, grammar, formatting, speech-to-text, and text-to-speech tools that do not generate or substantially rewrite expressive content.
- Prohibited submission of members' work, critique, workshop discussion, private communications, or other server material to AI systems.
- Updated the Community Standards, Member Guide, README, and channel proposal to reflect the policy and the human-to-human nature of creative exchange.
